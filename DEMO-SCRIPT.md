## 🎯 **Voice Over Tips:**

### Tone & Style:
- **Professional yet friendly**
- **Enthusiastic but not over-the-top**
- **Clear and confident**
- **Moderate pace** (not too fast)

### Emphasis Words:
- **AI-powered** / **Claude AI**
- **Automatically**
- **Real-time**
- **97% conversion probability**
- **Personalized**
- **Production-grade**

### Pacing:
- Speak at **140-150 words per minute**
- Pause briefly after each feature
- Give viewers time to see the UI

---

## 🎤 **Voice Over Recording Tips:**

1. **Environment:**
   - Quiet room with no echo
   - Close curtains (reduce reverb)
   - Use a good quality microphone

2. **Settings for Female Voice:**
   - Clear, professional tone
   - Warm but authoritative
   - Slightly energetic (not monotone)

3. **Recording Software:**
   - **Audacity** (free, easy to use)
   - **Adobe Audition** (professional)
   - **Descript** (with AI enhancement)

4. **Post-Processing:**
   - Remove background noise
   - Normalize audio levels
   - Add slight compression
   - EQ to enhance clarity
